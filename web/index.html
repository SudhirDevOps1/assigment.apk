How to get your printed copy

  1. Open a subject and press Print. Every subject page has a Print / Save A4 button. In the print dialog choose your printer, set Margins: Default, turn Background graphics ON, and switch Headers and footers OFF.
  2. Before printing, type your name and roll number on the cover page. They are filled in automatically on every page of that subject.
  3. For your own handwriting, use the page markers: each printed page begins with a small Page 1 of 3 badge, so you can never lose your place.
  4. On a phone, the same pages work inside the Android app with pinch zoom and focus mode — no network needed after install.

The whole suite is 39 A4 pages — 6 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 6. Every page is planned, never filler: if a page looks light, the room left is where your handwriting and diagrams go.
